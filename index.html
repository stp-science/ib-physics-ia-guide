Final submission format

  • Submit the final report as a PDF.
  • Maximum 3000 words.
  • Use a readable format: 11–12 pt Arial and sensible spacing; 1.5 line spacing is recommended.
  • Use a descriptive title, not simply “Physics IA”.
  • A title page and table of contents are not required.

What must be on the final version?

  • Do not put your name inside the final report.
  • Include your student code, word count and page numbers.
  • Number figures, tables and graphs and refer to them in your writing.
  • Use correct units and make graphs large enough to read.
  • Reference accepted values, external diagrams and sources appropriately.

Draft feedback

Your teacher can give advice on one draft. Feedback can tell you how the work could be improved, but the teacher cannot edit the IA for you. The version submitted after that feedback should be your final version.

Authenticity and AI

The IA must be your own work. Use AI cautiously and follow school and IB requirements. AI-generated physics, background information or evaluation is not an authoritative source: verify the physics yourself and cite the original sources you actually use.

Choosing an investigation

Simple apparatus, thoughtful question.

A common experiment is not automatically weak. The aim is to use a manageable setup but ask a question that gives you something meaningful to analyse and evaluate.

Avoid: making the investigation complicated just to appear original. A clear single independent variable and reliable quantitative data are more useful.
See the seven suggested practical families →

Before your teacher approves your question

  1. 1
    One clear independent variable
    Something you can deliberately change over a sensible range.
  2. 2
    One measurable dependent variable
    Measured quantitatively with suitable precision.
  3. 3
    A physics model
    You should know what relationship or behaviour theory predicts.
  4. 4
    Enough data
    Plan several values of the independent variable and repeated measurements.
  5. 5
    A graph with a purpose
    Know what you expect to plot before collecting the full dataset.

Remember

Your data drives the rest of the IA.

A sophisticated evaluation cannot rescue unreliable data. Pilot first, refine the method, then collect enough quality data to trust your conclusion.
