Suggested workflow

When should I use each page?

1Before the experimentUse Research Design to get the question and method right.
2After data collectionUse Data Analysis while processing, graphing and calculating uncertainty.
3Once analysis is completeUse Conclusion to answer the RQ from the evidence.
4Finish by evaluatingUse Evaluation to judge the method using evidence from your results.
Do not write the IA in a completely linear way. Your analysis may reveal that you need to revisit your theory or explain a method choice more clearly. The four pages are separate for navigation, but the investigation should still read as one coherent report.
